Target Identification
Yr 1-2
Identify a biological target (protein, gene) implicated in disease. Validate that modulating it will have therapeutic effect. Genomics, proteomics, and AI accelerating this stage significantly.
Lead Optimization
Yr 2-4
Medicinal chemistry iterates on hit compounds to improve potency, selectivity, and drug-like properties (ADMET). AlphaFold and generative AI now dramatically compress this phase by predicting binding geometries.
Preclinical Studies
Yr 4-6 · $100M
In vitro and in vivo studies establish safety and preliminary efficacy before human exposure. IND application filed with FDA. ~50% of Phase I failures were predictable from preclinical data in retrospect.
Phase I — III Trials
Yr 6-12 · $1B+
Phase I tests safety in 20-100 healthy volunteers. Phase II tests efficacy in 100-500 patients. Phase III confirms at scale in 1,000-5,000 patients across multiple sites. Phase III success rate: ~58% for oncology.
NDA / BLA + Approval
Yr 12-15 · $500M
New Drug Application submitted to FDA. Standard review takes 10-12 months. Priority Review: 6 months. Breakthrough designation can cut 2+ years from the overall timeline. Post-marketing surveillance continues indefinitely.
