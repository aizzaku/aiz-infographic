SCIENCE MAP
Aging is no longer considered an inevitable fate. The longevity field has shifted from observational gerontology to interventional biology — identifying the molecular mechanisms of aging and engineering ways to slow, halt, or reverse them. This is where the science stands in 2025.

SENOLYTICS & DRUGS
Rapamycin (mTOR inhibitor)
Most consistent lifespan extension in mammals. Extends median lifespan 10-15% in mice. Human trials ongoing.
Dasatinib + Quercetin
First senolytic combo. Clears senescent cells. Mayo Clinic trials show improved physical function in IPF patients.
Metformin (TAME trial)
Largest aging trial in history. Testing if the diabetes drug delays age-related diseases across 3,000 subjects.
NAD+ precursors (NMN/NR)
Restore declining NAD+ levels. David Sinclair's group shows reversal of vascular aging in mice.
Ge
Genetic
GENE THERAPY
Yamanaka factor reprogramming
Partial reprogramming with OSKM factors resets epigenetic age without de-differentiation. Altos Labs focus area.
Telomere extension (TERT)
BioViva's AAV-delivered TERT therapy extended telomeres in Elizabeth Parrish. Early-stage, no peer-reviewed data.
p16 clearance
Targeted removal of p16Ink4a+ senescent cells restores tissue function in mouse aging models. Unity Biotechnology.
Klotho gene delivery
Klotho protein declines with age. AAV-delivered Klotho improves cognition and kidney function in aged mice.
Bio
Biomarkers
AGING CLOCKS
Horvath DNA methylation clock
Measures biological age from methylation patterns. 353 CpG sites predict age within 3.6 years across tissue types.
GrimAge / PhenoAge
Mortality-predictive clocks. GrimAge outperforms chronological age for predicting time-to-death. Used in interventional trials.
Proteomics clocks (SomaScan)
Plasma protein panels that track organ-specific aging. 2023 Stanford study found organs age asynchronously.
Transcriptomic age
RNA expression patterns as aging biomarkers. Less stable than methylation but captures dynamic regulatory changes.
AI
AI-Assisted
COMPUTATIONAL
AlphaFold for aging targets
Protein structure prediction accelerates drug discovery for aging-related pathways — mTOR, sirtuins, senescence.
Foundation models for longevity
Insilico Medicine used generative AI to design a senolytic drug candidate in 18 months. Now in Phase II trial.
Multi-omic aging prediction
ML models integrating methylomics, proteomics, metabolomics for composite biological age scores.
Digital twins for aging
Personalized organ-level simulations predict intervention outcomes. Paradigm still early but well-funded.

Key Research Milestones
2013
Hallmarks Published
Lopez-Otin et al. define 9 hallmarks of aging in Cell. Becomes the field's organizing framework.
2016
Yamanaka Reprogramming
Salk Institute shows partial OSKM reprogramming reverses aging markers in mice without tumor formation.
2019
Thymus Regeneration
Volunteers in TRIIM trial show 2.5-year epigenetic age reversal using GH, DHEA, and metformin combination.
2021
Altos Labs Founded
$3B raised from Jeff Bezos and others to fund reprogramming biology. Recruits Shinya Yamanaka as advisor.
2023
Organ Aging Clocks
Stanford proteomics study shows organs age at different rates. "Brain ager" vs "heart ager" as distinct risk profiles.
2025
First AI Drug in Ph.II
Insilico Medicine's AI-designed anti-fibrosis drug enters Phase II — first AI-discovered longevity-adjacent compound in humans.
