# Social Media Copy Generation

Companion copy for infographic distribution across social platforms. Triggered after export when the infographic serves marketing/growth purposes.

## When to Generate

- Growth-hacking, marketing, or business-focused infographics
- User explicitly requests social media copy
- Content is data-driven with shareable insights
- After successful export (§8) but before §8.5 standalone extraction

## Platform Templates

### X/Twitter (280 chars + link)
**Pattern:** Hook + 2-3 key stats + Call-to-action + Link

```
🚨 [Compelling hook about the crisis/opportunity]: [Top stat], [Second stat], and [Third stat]. [Brief implication]. 

📊 Full data: [source_link]
```

**Character budget:** 
- Hook: ~80 chars
- Stats: ~120 chars  
- CTA: ~40 chars
- Link: ~40 chars
- Total: ~280 chars

### LinkedIn (1300 chars)
**Pattern:** Problem statement + Bulleted key findings + Business implications + Discussion prompt + Hashtags

```
[Opening problem/trend statement in 1-2 sentences.]

Here's what the data shows:
• [Stat 1 with context]
• [Stat 2 with context]  
• [Stat 3 with context]
• [Stat 4 with context]
• [Notable insight with specific numbers]

[2-3 sentences on business implications and strategic recommendations.]

[Discussion question prompt]

Source: [Attribution]

#[6-8 relevant hashtags]
```

### Instagram Stories/Carousel Copy
**Pattern:** Slide-by-slide narrative with emoji bullets

```
Slide 1: 🚨 [Crisis/opportunity hook]
Slide 2: 📊 By the numbers: [3-4 key stats]
Slide 3: 💡 What this means: [Implications]
Slide 4: 🎯 What to do: [Actionable takeaways]
```

## Copy Rules

- **Lead with the most shocking/compelling stat** - Use the hero number from the infographic
- **Include specific numbers** - "60%" not "most", "2.5% decline" not "dropping"  
- **Cite sources** - Always include attribution to maintain credibility
- **Use platform-native formatting** - Bullets for LinkedIn, emoji for Instagram, concise for Twitter
- **Add relevant hashtags** - Research-backed hashtags for the vertical (SEO, GrowthHacking, etc.)
- **Include CTA** - Drive to source link, ask for engagement, or prompt discussion

## Generation Process

1. **Extract key stats** from the infographic (usually 3-5 numbers)
2. **Identify the hook** - What's the most compelling/alarming insight?
3. **Frame business implications** - Why should the audience care?
4. **Adapt to platform constraints** - Respect character limits and formatting norms
5. **Add proper attribution** - Match the infographic's source citations

## Quality Checks

- [ ] Hook creates urgency or curiosity within first 10 words
- [ ] 3+ specific numbers included with context  
- [ ] Platform character limits respected
- [ ] Source attribution matches infographic
- [ ] Hashtags are relevant and researched (not generic)
- [ ] Call-to-action is clear and specific
